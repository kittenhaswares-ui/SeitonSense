# Privacy

Seiton Sense does not create accounts, contact a server, upload gameplay data,
or store combat history. While active, it transiently reads your current job,
PvP duty, visible enemy slots, positions, HP, and Seiton usability from the
local FFXIV client. It does not read character names or Home Worlds, and none
of that gameplay data is persisted. Only your local display settings are saved
through Dalamud.
